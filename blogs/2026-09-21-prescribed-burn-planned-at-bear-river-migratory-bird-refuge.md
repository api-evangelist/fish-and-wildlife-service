BRIGHAM CITY, Utah – Weather and national preparedness levels permitting, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service plans to conduct a prescribed burn on Unit 2C at Bear River Migratory Bird Refuge during the week of Sept. 21–25, 2026.
