Cyclists from across Kansas and Nebraska gather annually at Kirwin National Wildlife Refuge for Take a Ride on the Wild Side, a recreational biking event that combines outdoor adventure, wildlife viewing and an opportunity to experience the natural beauty of north-central Kansas.
