DUGWAY, Utah – Weather and site conditions permitting, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and the U.S. Wildland Fire Service plans to conduct a prescribed burn in the Harrison Unit at Fish Springs National Wildlife Refuge during October 2026, which will result in a temporary closure.
