It can be hard to imagine that a beautiful wild place could exist so near the concrete, steel, and groomed gardens of a vast metropolitan area like Detroit, Michigan, and its suburbs. But Detroit River International Wildlife Refuge does exist, offering wonder and the glory of nature to all visitors.
